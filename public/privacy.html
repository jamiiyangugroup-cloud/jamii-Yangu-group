Privacy notice for accounts and membership applications

This draft describes the Supabase-backed account and application flow in this project. It is not a final privacy policy. Do not enable public intake until the cooperative confirms its controller identity, lawful bases, processors, retention schedule, data-subject request channel, security controls and cross-border processing details with a qualified Kenyan data protection adviser.

Information collected

Supabase Auth handles account email and password authentication; Jamii Yangu does not store passwords in its application tables. After email verification and sign-in, the membership form requests your full legal name, date of birth, optional gender, phone number, county, sub-county, physical address, occupation, next-of-kin name and phone, and consent timestamp. The form currently does not collect national ID numbers, photos, uploaded documents or payments.

Purpose and status

The intended purpose is to create a login and receive a prospective membership application. An application is not an approved membership. No registration fee is charged by this website and no financial product or investment is offered through this flow.

Storage and access

Application records are intended to be stored in the cooperative's Supabase PostgreSQL project and accessed under database Row Level Security. Account credentials are handled by Supabase Auth. The project owner must select the data region, configure production email delivery, finish and test access policies, set retention and backup procedures, and publish a monitored privacy contact before real intake.

Documents and other services

Private storage buckets and server-side upload/download functions are scaffolded, but document intake is disabled and no document upload control is active. Payments, SMS, notifications and financial records are not connected to this public application flow.

Your choices and requests

Required fields are needed to submit an application. The production notice must provide a monitored contact for access, correction, deletion, objection and complaint requests, and describe the applicable rights and response process.

Contact

Verified controller identity and privacy contact details have not been configured. This draft is not ready for operational use.
