Membership application terms

Submitting an application asks Jamii Yangu Group Cooperative to review your request to join. Submission does not make you a member, approve your application, create an account, or establish eligibility for financial assistance.

The cooperative may request identity documents or more information through a separate secure process. Any membership fee, payment channel, contribution obligation, or effective date must be presented to you and accepted before payment is requested. This application page does not collect money.

Decisions

Applications are subject to the cooperative's membership rules and authorized review. A submitted application is not a promise of approval or access to financial services.
